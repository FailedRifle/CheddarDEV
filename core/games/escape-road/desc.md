# Escape Road

This high-octane driving evasion game plunges you into a relentless police chase where you must dodge cops, weave through traffic, and survive as long as possible. Snagging cash drops lets you unlock sturdier vehicles to extend your run.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Fluid handling during chaotic chases.
* **Load Time:** Rapid vehicle selection and spawning.
* **Latency:** Tight, responsive steering controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
