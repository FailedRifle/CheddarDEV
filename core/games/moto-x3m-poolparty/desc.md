# Moto X3M Pool Party

This sun-soaked spin-off of the classic stunt racing series drops your dirt bike onto vibrant, water-themed tracks filled with giant slides, massive jumps, and explosive hazards. You must master air-rotation controls to pull off perfect flips and survive hazardous pool obstacles.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Fluid physics rendering for high-speed stunts.
* **Load Time:** Rapid level reloading for instant retry loops.
* **Latency:** Responsive throttle, brake, and bike-tilt inputs.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
