# POSTAL

A dark, psychological isometric shooter where you play as the Postal Dude, fighting your way through surreal, hostile environments as a violent breakdown unfolds around you. 

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable and acceptable frame rates.
* **Load Time:** Rapid execution for immediate browser-based gameplay access.
* **Latency:** Responsive and dependable performance.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
