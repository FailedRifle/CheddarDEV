# Brawl Simulator 3D

This action-packed 3D fighting simulation game pits you against waves of opponents in stylized arena combat. You utilize special abilities, melee strikes, and tactical movement to dominate the arena and emerge victorious.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth arena rendering during fast-paced combat.
* **Load Time:** Rapid match initialization.
* **Latency:** Responsive combat and movement inputs.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
