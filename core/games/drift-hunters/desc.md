# Drift Hunters

This immersive driving and tuning game lets you slide custom sports cars around detailed tracks to rack up massive drift combos. You use earned cash to upgrade engine parts, tune handling, and buy new vehicles.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth rendering for high-speed slides.
* **Load Time:** Moderate map loading times.
* **Latency:** Precise steering and throttle feedback.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
