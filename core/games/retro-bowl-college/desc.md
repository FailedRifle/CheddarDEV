# Retro Bowl College

This retro-style American football game puts you in charge of a college program. Recruit and develop players, build a winning team, and take control on the field as you compete for the championship.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth retro-style gameplay.
* **Load Time:** Quick startup for fast access to the game.
* **Latency:** Responsive passing and running controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
