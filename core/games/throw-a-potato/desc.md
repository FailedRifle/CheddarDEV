# Throw a Potato

This humorous physics projectile game tasks you with launching a humble tuber across ridiculous distances using customizable cannons and mid-air boosters. Upgrading your launch power and aerodynamic traits maximizes your distance records.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Fluid arc physics tracking.
* **Load Time:** Instant restart capability.
* **Latency:** Responsive launch angle adjustments.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
