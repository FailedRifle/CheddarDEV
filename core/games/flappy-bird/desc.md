# Flappy Bird

This notoriously punishing arcade game challenges you to guide a small, flapping bird safely through endless gaps between green pipes. Every single tap propels your bird upward against gravity, where a single miscalculation or graze against an obstacle ends your run instantly.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth and locked for precise obstacle timing.
* **Load Time:** Instantaneous startup for quick retries.
* **Latency:** Zero-delay tap response for split-second navigation.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
