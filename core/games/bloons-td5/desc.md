# Bloons Tower Defense 5

This refined tower defense strategy game expands on the classic formula with deeper monkey paths, special agents, and diverse daily challenges. You balance economy and defense to pop every single incoming balloon before it breaches the exit.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth performance during heavy projectile scaling.
* **Load Time:** Rapid level loading.
* **Latency:** Immediate tower upgrade and targeting feedback.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
