# Grand Theft Auto III

This pioneering open-world action game drops you into the sprawling, crime-ridden streets of Liberty City following a betrayal during a bank heist. You climb the criminal underworld ranks by taking on contract jobs for various mafia families, corrupt cops, and street gangs.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable engine performance for open-world urban streaming.
* **Load Time:** Moderate asset loading for district transitions.
* **Latency:** Dependable third-person combat and driving handling.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
