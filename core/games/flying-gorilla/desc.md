# Flying Gorilla

This chaotic physics-based arcade game challenges you to guide a jetpack-wearing gorilla through treacherous jungle obstacles and floating hazards. You flap your wings and tap the boost to maintain momentum and dodge obstacles without crashing.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth and stable for fast-paced arcade movement.
* **Load Time:** Instant startup for immediate gameplay.
* **Latency:** Responsive tap and tilt controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
