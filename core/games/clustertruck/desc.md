# ClusterTruck

This chaotic first-person platformer drops you onto the roofs of speeding, crashing semi-trucks that are constantly destroying themselves around you. You must leap across the moving vehicles without touching the ground to reach the finish line.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** High and stable to handle intense physics chaos.
* **Load Time:** Rapid checkpoint reloading.
* **Latency:** Ultra-responsive jump and air-strafe controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
