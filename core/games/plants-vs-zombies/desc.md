# Plants VS. Zombies

This strategic tower defense game deploys a specialized army of botanical flora to protect a suburban home from waves of quirky, advancing zombies. Sun resource management dictates your line of defense against specialized undead variants.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth grid-based action rendering.
* **Load Time:** Quick level initialization.
* **Latency:** Immediate seed packet planting response.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
