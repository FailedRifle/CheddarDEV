# Flappy Dunk

This arcade sports game combines classic flap-based mechanics with basketball shooting, requiring you to guide a bouncing ball continuously through descending hoops. Every successful swish rewards precise timing and momentum control to maintain your streak without hitting the ground.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth and stable for continuous ball tracking.
* **Load Time:** Instant execution for immediate game restarts.
* **Latency:** Responsive tap and hold controls for arc adjustments.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
