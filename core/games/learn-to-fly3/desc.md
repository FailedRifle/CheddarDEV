# Learn to Fly 3

This physics launcher game follows a determined penguin trying to reach outer space using homemade sleds, boosters, and experimental rockets. You upgrade your components across repeated launch runs to break through atmospheric barriers.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Fluid flight physics rendering.
* **Load Time:** Rapid launch-pad turnaround.
* **Latency:** Responsive mid-air booster controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
