# Retro Bowl

This stylized American football management game puts you in control of a struggling franchise's front office and on-field playcalling. You draft players, manage team morale, and execute precise passing routes to win the championship title.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Consistent retro-style performance.
* **Load Time:** Instant game-state loading.
* **Latency:** Snappy passing and running route inputs.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
