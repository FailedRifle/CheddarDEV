# Super Mario 64

This landmark 3D platformer explores expansive, free-roaming worlds inside magical paintings within Princess Peach's castle. You collect Power Stars by mastering fluid movement mechanics like long jumps, wall kicks, and dives.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable console-accurate framerate.
* **Load Time:** Fast painting-world transitions.
* **Latency:** Ultra-responsive acrobatic controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
