# AdVenture Capitalist!

This capitalist idle simulation game starts you out with a single lemon stand and challenges you to build a massive, multi-industry business empire. By automating investments, hiring managers, and aggressively scaling your profits, you generate astronomical sums of wealth to conquer the global market.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth and optimized for idle mechanics.
* **Load Time:** Quick initial startup for continuous background progression.
* **Latency:** Low, seamless tracking for offline and active earnings.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
