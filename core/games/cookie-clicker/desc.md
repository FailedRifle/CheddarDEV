# COOKIE CLICKER

This addictive incremental game challenges players to produce an astronomical quantity of cookies, starting with a single click and scaling up through automated bakeries, grandmas, and interdimensional portals. Optimization and strategic investment drive your progression toward infinite yield.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth rendering with minor drops during high particle load.
* **Load Time:** Instantaneous launch directly in your browser.
* **Latency:** Local execution ensures zero input delay.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported (single-player offline progression).
