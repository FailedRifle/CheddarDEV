# Raft Wars 2

This whimsical artillery puzzle game arms two brothers on a raft with tennis ball and rocket launchers to defend their waterpark gold from greedy thieves. You adjust trajectory and power to sink enemy rafts in turn-based combat.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable projectile physics.
* **Load Time:** Fast stage loading.
* **Latency:** Smooth drag-and-fire controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
