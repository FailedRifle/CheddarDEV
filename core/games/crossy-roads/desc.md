# Crossy Road

This frantic arcade hopper challenges you to guide a pixelated character across an endless maze of busy roads, rushing rivers, and train tracks. You must time your forward hops and side-to-side dodges perfectly to avoid getting hit while collecting coins to unlock quirky new characters.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** High and stable frame rate for fast-paced pixel movement.
* **Load Time:** Rapid character selection and level generation.
* **Latency:** Zero-delay tap and swipe controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Supported via local multiplayer modes.
