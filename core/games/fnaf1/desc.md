# Five Nights at Freddy's

This iconic survival horror game puts you in the shoes of a night security guard trapped in a pizzeria with animatronic characters that roam the halls. You must carefully track their movements through security cameras and manage strictly limited power supplies to keep the doors shut.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable, fixed-perspective performance.
* **Load Time:** Quick night initialization.
* **Latency:** Immediate input reaction for power conservation.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
