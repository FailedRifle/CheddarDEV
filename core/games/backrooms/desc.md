# Backrooms

This atmospheric survival horror game traps you within an endless labyrinth of monotonous, yellow-tinted office rooms. You must navigate through the disorienting maze, manage your stamina, and avoid unseen entities while searching for a way out of the surreal geometry.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Consistent frame rates tailored for exploration.
* **Load Time:** Fast generation for procedural room loading.
* **Latency:** Responsive movement controls for tight corridors.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
