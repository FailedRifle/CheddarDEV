# BOWMASTERS

This chaotic and colorful projectile game tests your aiming precision as you launch wacky characters, weapons, and quirky objects across the screen to hit opponents. Featuring ballistic physics, strategic trajectory calculation, and a massive roster of over-the-top characters with unique special attacks, every match requires careful angle and power adjustments.

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth and fluid performance.
* **Load Time:** Rapid execution for immediate gameplay access.
* **Latency:** Responsive and dependable performance.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
