# Five Nights at Freddy's 4

This atmospheric, bedroom-based horror game strips away cameras, forcing you to rely entirely on acute audio cues to survive. You must listen closely at doors, flash lights down the hallway, and manage closet threats in the dark.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Consistent framerate tailored for audio-visual synchronization.
* **Load Time:** Fast load into the bedroom environment.
* **Latency:** Crisp spatial audio tracking and door reaction times.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
