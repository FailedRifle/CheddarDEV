# Snow Rider 3D

This fast-paced downhill sledding game challenges you to navigate snowy mountain tracks filled with sharp turns, giant snowballs, and wooden obstacles. Collecting scattered gift boxes lets you unlock faster sleds.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Fluid motion rendering at high speeds.
* **Load Time:** Quick track startup.
* **Latency:** Responsive steering and jump timing.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
