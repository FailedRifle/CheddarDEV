# 60 SECONDS!

This dark comedy survival game drops you into a fallout shelter with only sixty seconds to scavenge supplies before a nuclear apocalypse hits. Every item you grab and every family member you save (or leave behind) shapes how long you'll last underground, forcing tough calls when time is the one thing you don't have enough of.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable and consistent.
* **Load Time:** Rapid execution for immediate gameplay access.
* **Latency:** Responsive and dependable performance.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
