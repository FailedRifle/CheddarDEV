# Geometry Dash Lite

This rhythm-based platforming game challenges you to time precise jumps and flips to the beat of an electronic soundtrack. A single misstep on a spike or block resets your run instantly from the very beginning.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** High, locked framerate for precise timing.
* **Load Time:** Instant level restart capability.
* **Latency:** Zero input lag for split-second jumps.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
