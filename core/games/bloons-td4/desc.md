# Bloons Tower Defense 4

This classic strategy tower defense game tasks you with stopping relentless waves of colorful balloons by strategically placing specialized monkey towers and upgrades along the track. Every placement and path choice dictates whether your defense holds up.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable even during high-density balloon waves.
* **Load Time:** Fast initialization.
* **Latency:** Precise placement and targeting responsiveness.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
