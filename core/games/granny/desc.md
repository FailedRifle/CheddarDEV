# Granny

This terrifying first-person survival horror game traps you inside a dark, creaky house controlled by a hostile, baseball-bat-wielding grandmother. You must scavenge for escape tools, solve puzzles, and move silently through the rooms while avoiding her relentless hunts over a strict five-day limit.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable frame rates optimized for dark atmospheric environments.
* **Load Time:** Fast initialization for immediate house exploration.
* **Latency:** Responsive stealth movement and item interaction controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
