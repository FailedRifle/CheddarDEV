# Nazi Zombies

This iconic cooperative round-based survival mode pits players against endless waves of Nazi zombies inside confined, barricaded structures. You open doors, buy wall weapons, and spin the Mystery Box to stay alive.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth action performance during heavy round swarms.
* **Load Time:** Moderate map asset loading.
* **Latency:** Responsive first-person shooting mechanics.
* **Multiplayer:** Supported.
