# BUCKSHOT ROULETTE

This high-stakes variation of classic Russian roulette replaces the traditional revolver with a 12-gauge pump-action shotgun. The weapon is loaded with a combination of live rounds and blanks, requiring careful calculation and strategic decision-making when selecting targets.
