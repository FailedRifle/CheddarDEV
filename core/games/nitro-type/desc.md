# Nitro Type

This fast-paced competitive typing game pits you against real players in high-speed car races where your typing speed and accuracy directly power your vehicle. You practice keying strings of text as quickly as possible to boost your ride, earn cash, and unlock custom cars.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth and stable during track animations.
* **Load Time:** Rapid lobby matching and race initialization.
* **Latency:** Zero-delay keystroke registration for competitive typing.
* **Multiplayer:** Supported.
