# Five Nights at Freddy's 2

This intense sequel expands the survival horror formula by removing security doors and introducing a flashlight, a Freddy mask, and a music box. You must manage a barrage of aggressive new animatronics crawling through vents and creeping down the main hallway.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth camera and mask-wearing transitions.
* **Load Time:** Rapid night loading.
* **Latency:** Responsive mask toggle and music box winding.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
