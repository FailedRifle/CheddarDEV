# Cheese Rolling

This physics-driven sports game challenges you to chase a rolling wheel of cheese down a dangerously steep and bumpy hill. You must balance momentum, dodge hazards, and survive brutal tumbles to reach the bottom in one piece.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Stable physics calculation rate.
* **Load Time:** Fast track generation.
* **Latency:** Responsive leaning and steering inputs.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
