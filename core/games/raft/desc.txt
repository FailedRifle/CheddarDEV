# Raft

This open-world ocean survival game strands you on a tiny square of debris with nothing more than a plastic hook in the middle of a vast, endless sea. You must snag floating debris, expand your floating home into a sprawling vessel, filter clean water, and fend off a relentless shark to survive.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth rendering for open water and modular building physics.
* **Load Time:** Moderate world generation and resource seeding.
* **Latency:** Responsive first-person survival and hook-throwing mechanics.
* **Multiplayer:** Supported via online co-op.
