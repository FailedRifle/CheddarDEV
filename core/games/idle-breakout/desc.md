# Idle Breakout

This modern idle twist on the classic brick-breaker arcade formula automates paddle and ball mechanics while introducing massive upgrade trees. You buy specialized balls and damage upgrades to obliterate dense screens of blocks automatically.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth execution during heavy multi-ball calculations.
* **Load Time:** Instantaneous save-state loading.
* **Latency:** Immediate upgrade response.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
