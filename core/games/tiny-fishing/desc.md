# Tiny Fishing

This addictive arcade fishing game challenges you to cast your line deep into the ocean to catch a wide variety of colorful and rare fish. You use earned cash from your catches to upgrade your hook depth, line strength, and passive earnings to haul in bigger treasures.

---

### Performance & Specifications

* **FPS:** Smooth and stable for water and catch animations.
* **Load Time:** Instantaneous startup for quick sessions.
* **Latency:** Responsive drag-and-drop casting controls.
* **Multiplayer:** Unsupported.
