# CheddarOS™
## Privacy Policy

#### Information We Collect
When you create an account, CheddarOS collects only:

* **Your username** — the one you choose when creating your account.
* **Your Google account information** — limited to what Google provides during sign-in to verify it's you (such as your Google account ID and email address), used to connect your account and confirm your identity on future sign-ins.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or any other personal data beyond what's listed above.

#### What We Don't Do
* **We do not sell your information.** Your username and Google account information are never sold, rented, or traded to any third party, for any reason.
* We do not share this information with advertisers or data brokers.

#### How We Use This Information
* To create and secure your account.
* To verify your identity when you sign in, by confirming the Google account you're signing in with matches the one linked to your username.
* To enforce the Terms of Service, including identifying accounts for moderation purposes (see **Account Bans & Enforcement** below).

#### Account Bans & Enforcement
As outlined in the **Terms of Service**, accounts found to be abusing CheddarOS, violating platform rules, or created in bad faith may be **permanently banned**. Your username and linked Google account information may be used internally to identify and enforce bans and to prevent banned accounts from being recreated. This information is not disclosed publicly as part of enforcement, beyond what the Terms of Service already allow (such as a public ban reason, where applicable).

#### Data Retention
We retain your username and Google account information for as long as your account exists. If your account is deleted, this information is removed from active use, except where retaining limited records (such as ban history) is necessary to enforce the Terms of Service.

#### Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated as CheddarOS changes. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

By creating an account and using **CheddarOS**, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the terms above.
